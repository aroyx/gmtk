# Echoes of Autumn 

A topper girl got failed in an exam, now she has to travel from the staffroom to her classroom to put her marksheet in her bag but without getting caught by her classmates , otherwise she'll get bullied for getting failed.

this game is a submission for GMTK 2026 GameJam and [jamegam](https://jamegam.hackclub.com/).
